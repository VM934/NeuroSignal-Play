# NeuroSignal: Signal Recovery

An original first-person game for **one to three Windows players**. Protect a fictional neuroscience research archive from drones. Clear three waves and check three records to win.

## Download and play

1. Open the [latest Windows release](https://github.com/VM934/NeuroSignal-Play/releases/latest). Under Assets, download **NeuroSignal-SignalRecovery-Windows-20261107-202503.zip** for **0.7.0-playtest.1**.
2. Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click **Play NeuroSignal.cmd**.
3. Choose **Deploy solo**, or use **Host co-op** and **Join a squadmate**.

Playing requires no editor, compiler, account or AI subscription. The launchers select **1080p, 100% 3D render scale, FXAA, GI quality 1 and a 30 FPS cap**. The ZIP includes controls, prerequisites, a source-linked learning guide and file hashes.

## Play the mission

- **Fight for the archive.** When **DRONE LOCK** appears, get behind solid cover. Hold Shift to sprint and hold C or Ctrl to slide. Try the marked **LOW ROUTE** on the north side as a shortcut. Let go of aim to resume a sprint you're still holding. **ARCHIVE TARGETED** signals a shot at the objective.
- **Decide what the records support.** Use the central console after each wave. Decide how much access an app actually needs, examine a faulty brain recording, and compare a fictional compound with its matched carrier before accepting a drug claim.
- **Practice an everyday choice.** After a correct answer, press **Tab / View** to open Field Notes. Select Science or Cybersecurity, then **F / X** to open a Field Drill. Read the explanation, retry if needed, and mark yourself ready separately. The drills do not advance the squad.

The six drills cover trustworthy measurements, caffeine and sleep, suspected opioid overdose, unexpected device codes, unsafe instructions in documents sent to AI assistants, and restoring from a backup. The mission data are fictional. **LEARNING-GUIDE-2026.md** links the source material and the dated examples.

## Play with friends or build on the project

Everyone needs the **same complete 0.7.0-playtest.1 release**. One player hosts, and up to two join the host's reachable IPv4 address. The default port is **UDP 7777**. Co-op uses direct IP. Separate-home Internet play requires a network path to the host and has not been verified.

The private team release pairs the identical executable with the editable project. Sign in with your invited account and work on your own branch. C++ development uses **Unreal Engine 5.8.3** and Windows C++ tools.
