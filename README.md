# NeuroSignal: Signal Recovery

![Signal Recovery 3D gameplay](https://github.com/VM934/NeuroSignal-Play/releases/latest/download/combat.png)

A 3D first-person game for Windows, playable solo or cooperatively with up to two friends. Defend a fictional research archive, slide beneath low cover, fight drones and recover a trustworthy reference record. Investigate app permissions, neural recordings and experimental controls, then try everyday decisions about medicines, scams and backups.

**[Download the Windows game](https://github.com/VM934/NeuroSignal-Play/releases/latest).** Extract the complete ZIP and open **Play NeuroSignal.cmd**. Choose **Deploy solo**, **Host co-op** or **Join a squadmate**.
